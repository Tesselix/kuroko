# Fonts

The `.krkde` atlases in this folder are rendered from **Inter** (Regular and
SemiBold) by Rasmus Andersson, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). Inter: https://rsms.me/inter/

They are generated by `sdk/tools/fontgen/gen_fonts.py` and stored as Kuroko
records (type 0x0101).

The `hebrew-*.krkde` atlases are rendered from **Noto Sans Hebrew** (Regular
and SemiBold, version 3.001) by the Noto Project Authors, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `OFL-NotoSansHebrew.txt`.
https://github.com/notofonts/hebrew
